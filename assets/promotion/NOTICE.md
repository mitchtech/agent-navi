# Agent Navi promotional previews

Created by Michael J. Mitchell / mitchtech for
<https://github.com/mitchtech/agent-navi>. These are labeled illustrated previews,
not recordings of native host execution. Their creation changes no source atlas
or recording. See [the manifest](manifest.json) for source hashes and artifacts.

Pet previews derive from the active Agent Navi artwork. Contributor rights in
that artwork are licensed under CC BY 4.0, with its original provenance and
revision history in [the pet notice](../../pets/navi/NOTICE.md) and
[artwork license](../../pets/navi/LICENSE.txt). Composition adds captions,
resizes preview frames, and assembles them into videos; it does not create a
new native pet revision.

The sound-preview video incorporates Nintendo-derived Navi recordings from
The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Those recordings are excluded from the
MIT and CC BY licenses. Their [audio notice](../../audio/NOTICE.md) records
provenance and is not a grant of permission from Nintendo. Third-party character
rights and trademarks remain with their owners. No official affiliation is
claimed.

Titles and captions use the site's self-hosted fonts under the
[SIL Open Font License](../../site/fonts/OFL.md). The media-building script is
covered by the repository's code license. Keep these credits and notices with
redistributed preview files.
